Pharmacist credentialing

Get your pharmacists licensed, certified and enrolled with payers.

  • License tracking with IDFPR (the Illinois licensing agency) and NPI numbers (national provider numbers).
  • Certifications for immunization, contraception, test-and-treat and BLS (basic life support).
  • Certifications for HIV PrEP and PEP (prevention medicine taken before or after exposure).
  • Enrollment paperwork for Illinois Medicaid through IMPACT (the state's provider enrollment system).
  • Enrollment with HealthChoice Illinois plans (Medicaid managed care plans), CAQH (the shared credentialing database), commercial plans and Medicare.

Illinois now pays pharmacists for clinical care.

  • State-regulated Illinois health plans must cover pharmacist contraception and HIV PrEP and PEP services. This applies to plans issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2023.
  • They must also cover flu and COVID-19 vaccines, COVID-19 treatment and test-and-treat given by pharmacists. This applies to plans issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2025.
  • Coverage applies to in-network pharmacists at a negotiated rate.
  • Illinois Medicaid pays credentialed pharmacists for covered services. For example, HIV PrEP and PEP consultations pay 85 percent of the physician rate.
  • Payment depends on correct credentialing and documentation.
  • Payers make the final enrollment and payment decisions.
